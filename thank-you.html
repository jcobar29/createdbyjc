First task

First Task: Check Your Last 5 Posts

Hindi mo muna kailangang gumawa ng bagong content.

Balikan mo muna yung last 5 posts mo.

Inside The Big Aha, may simple audit na sasagutan mo:

  1. Ano yung purpose ng post na ’to?
  2. May nangyari ba after nilang makita? May nag-inquire, nag-click, or nag-take ng next step?

Baka mapansin mo na puro views at engagement yung goal ng posts mo, pero wala masyadong content na tumutulong sa audience mong mas maintindihan yung offer mo.

Kaya pala may nanonood, pero konti yung bumibili.

  1. Start with The Big Aha and audit your last 5 posts.
  2. After that, proceed to Find Your Content Gap para malaman mo kung saan ka dapat mag-focus.
  3. Then follow the roadmap one week at a time.
